Your captures stay with you.

The open-source desktop apps save captures locally. They do not require accounts or send captures to our servers.

On your desktop

Mac captures are saved in Pictures/Stillmark. Windows keeps local capture history and settings. Files are not automatically encrypted by Stillmark; your operating-system and disk security settings apply.

Screen recording and optional microphone access require system permissions. Text recognition uses the platform OCR implementation. Clipboard actions happen only when you choose them.

This website

This static site has no checkout, account form, analytics script, or Supabase browser client. Its hosting provider receives normal HTTP requests. GitHub receives requests when you follow source, release, issue, or support links.

Legacy customers

Records from the former commercial service are kept separately during migration. They are not part of the public source repository. Contact support@getstillmark.com for a private request about those records.
